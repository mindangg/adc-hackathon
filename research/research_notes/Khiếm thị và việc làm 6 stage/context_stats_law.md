# Visually impaired (VI) people and employment in Vietnam: statistics, law, organisations, business case (with global comparison)

Research date: 21 Sep 2026. Every figure below shows the date of its data where the source gives one.

## Q1. How many people in Vietnam are blind or visually impaired, and how many are of working age?

### Takeaway
The most-cited figure is **about 2 million blind or visually impaired people** in Vietnam. It comes from the Vietnam National Institute of Ophthalmology / Central Eye Hospital (2019, repeated in 2024). The official disability survey (NSO 2023) puts all disability at 6.11% of people aged 2+. I found no published count of working-age VI people.

### Cited Findings
- **Survey on People with Disabilities 2023 (NSO/GSO):** disability prevalence among people aged 2+ is **6.11%**, down 0.95 percentage points from 2016. It is 4.78% in urban areas and 6.89% in rural areas. Fieldwork ran 1 Sep–31 Oct 2023 and covered 73,125 households in 63 provinces. — [NSO press release, Nov 2024](https://www.nso.gov.vn/tin-tuc-thong-ke/2024/11/thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-ket-qua-dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat-nam-2023/). The full report is here: [PDF](https://www.nso.gov.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2023_-Bao-cao-Dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat.-pdf-1.pdf) and [publication page, Sep 2025](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/default/2025/09/results-of-the-survey-on-people-with-disabilities-2023/)
- **National Survey on People with Disabilities 2016 (VDS 2016, GSO with UNICEF):** just over 7% of people aged 2+ have a disability, about **6.2 million people**. A further 13% (nearly 12 million) live in a household with a disabled member. This was Vietnam's first large survey using international measurement standards (Washington Group functional questions). — [UN Viet Nam](https://vietnam.un.org/en/4053-launch-key-findings-viet-nam%E2%80%99s-first-large-scale-national-survey-people-disabilities-2016); [GSO page](https://www.gso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2019/03/vietnam-national-survey-on-people-with-disabilities-2016/)
- **About 2 million blind and visually impaired people** (Central Eye Hospital, reported Oct 2024):
  - About one-third of them are poor.
  - More than 80% of the blindness is preventable or treatable.
  - Cataract causes 66.1% of it.
  - About 500,000 people need corneal transplants.
  - — [Vietnam.vn, Oct 2024](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/viet-nam-hien-co-khoang-2-trieu-nguoi-mu-va-thi-luc-kem)
- The IAPB Vision Atlas cites the Vietnam National Institute of Ophthalmology (2019): **two million people in Vietnam live with vision impairment**. The main causes are cataract, uncorrected refractive error and retinal disease. The Atlas country page itself shows "No data available" for its modelled estimates (blindness, MSVI, productivity loss). — [IAPB Vision Atlas: Viet Nam](https://visionatlas.iapb.org/country-data/viet-nam/)
- 37.6% of Vietnamese schoolchildren have refractive errors. Myopia affects about 6 million children. — [IAPB Vision Atlas: Viet Nam](https://visionatlas.iapb.org/country-data/viet-nam/)
- **Vietnam Blind Association (Hội Người mù Việt Nam):** **72,697 members** in 28 provinces and cities, after the provincial merger of 1 Jul 2025. Reported at the association's 4th Congress. — [Nhân Dân, 17 Dec 2025](https://nhandan.vn/phan-dau-tao-viec-lam-nang-cao-chat-luong-doi-song-cho-nguoi-mu-post930923.html)
- **Global comparison (WHO):** at least **2.2 billion** people worldwide have near or distance vision impairment. At least **1 billion** of these cases could have been prevented or have not yet been addressed (WHO World Report on Vision, 2019). — [WHO fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/blindness-and-visual-impairment); [UN News, Oct 2019](https://news.un.org/en/story/2019/10/1048762)
- Vision impairment costs an estimated **US$411 billion (PPP) a year** in lost productivity worldwide. — [IAPB](https://www.iapb.world/annual_global_productivity_loss); [WHO fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/blindness-and-visual-impairment)

### Inferences
- The "2 million" figure is a clinical eye-health estimate: blind plus low vision, mostly older people with cataract. The Blind Association's 72,697 members are closer to the group of people who are "legally or functionally blind and organised". For a pitch, use both numbers and label them separately.
- Most of the 2 million are older people with treatable cataract. So the working-age, long-term VI population who need jobs support is much smaller, likely in the tens to low hundreds of thousands. This is an inference only; I found no source for it.

### Gaps
- I found no published count or share of VI people of working age (15–60/64) in Vietnam. The NSO 2023 full PDF may contain seeing-disability prevalence by age group, but I could not extract it. The press release does not give the vision-specific figure.
- I could not retrieve the VDS 2016 figure for seeing difficulty specifically.
- IAPB modelled estimates for Vietnam were not available on the Atlas page.

## Q2. How does employment of disabled and VI people in Vietnam compare with non-disabled people, what jobs do VI people typically do, and how does this compare globally?

### Takeaway
In Vietnam only **about 23.5% of PWD are working, against 76.3% of non-disabled people** (NSO 2023). For people with seeing disabilities the employment rate is **10% or below**. VI work is concentrated in massage and acupressure and in handicrafts, with low pay (about 2.3–3.6 million VND a month) and only 14.5% covered by social insurance. The employment rate for blind people is 27% in the UK and about 40% in the US.

### Cited Findings: Vietnam
- **NSO 2023, all disabilities compared with non-disabled:**

  | Indicator | People with disabilities | People without disabilities |
  |---|---|---|
  | Labour force participation | 23.9% | 77.4% (gap: 53.5 percentage points) |
  | Working | 23.5% | 76.3% |
  | Vocational training (age 15+) | 8.8% | 25.4% |
  | Internet use | 33.6% | 83.7% |
  | Mobile phone use | 53.7% | 89.2% |

  — [NSO press release, Nov 2024](https://www.nso.gov.vn/tin-tuc-thong-ke/2024/11/thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-ket-qua-dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat-nam-2023/)
- **NSO 2023, by type of disability:** people with mobility, hearing and cognitive disabilities have relatively high employment rates. People with **seeing**, communication, upper-body and psychosocial disabilities have employment rates of **10% or lower**. — [NSO press release (GSO mirror)](https://www.gso.gov.vn/tin-tuc-thong-ke/2024/11/thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-ket-qua-dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat-nam-2023/). This figure came through a search summary of the release; check it in the full [PDF](https://www.nso.gov.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2023_-Bao-cao-Dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat.-pdf-1.pdf) before putting it on a slide.
- **Data conflict:** one search summary said "31.7% of PWD aged 15+ employed vs 82.4%" and "63.8% not employed". I could not trace these numbers to a primary page. Use the press-release figures (23.5% against 76.3%) instead.
- Literacy of PWD aged 15+ was 79.0%, against 96.3% for non-disabled people (2023). — [NSO press release](https://www.nso.gov.vn/tin-tuc-thong-ke/2024/11/thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-ket-qua-dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat-nam-2023/) (via search summary)
- VDS 2016 found that adults with disabilities are less likely to be employed and more likely to be unemployed than their peers. — [UNICEF Viet Nam](https://www.unicef.org/vietnam/press-releases/launch-key-findings-viet-nams-first-large-scale-national-survey-people-disabilities)
- **Blind Association jobs ecosystem (2024 report, published 26 Feb 2025):**
  - **Facilities:** 371 concentrated production facilities (101 handicraft, 270 massage and acupressure), plus 137 small craft groups employing 4,099 people.
  - **Job creation in 2024:** 1,281 livelihood projects in 54 provinces created stable income for 2,533 workers.
  - **Income:** handicraft about 2.5 million VND a month, massage about 3.6 million VND a month, highly skilled workers 7–10 million VND a month.
  - **Social insurance:** only **14.5%** of blind workers take part.
  - — [Nhân Dân, Feb 2025](https://nhandan.vn/ty-le-lao-dong-mu-tham-gia-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-con-thap-post861853.html)
- A search summary also mentioned 1,087 self-managed massage groups with 3,462 workers, and ">90% employment" among trained massage workers. — [Nhân Dân / VBA sources via search](https://nhandan.vn/ty-le-lao-dong-mu-tham-gia-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-con-thap-post861853.html). I did not verify this on the primary page.
- **4th Congress of the Blind Association (Dec 2025):**
  - **Training, 2020–2025:** 489 vocational classes trained 6,871 people, with 24.211 billion VND in funding.
  - **Income:** handicraft about 2.3 million VND a month, massage about 3.5 million VND a month.
  - **Social support:** 56,000 members receive monthly social allowances.
  - **2025–2030 target:** **70% of working-age blind people in stable employment**.
  - — [Nhân Dân, 17 Dec 2025](https://nhandan.vn/phan-dau-tao-viec-lam-nang-cao-chat-luong-doi-song-cho-nguoi-mu-post930923.html)
- **Employer framing in HCMC (Sep 2026):** employers name telephone customer service, customer care and sales as suitable roles for VI workers, pointing to their "concentration and memory". — [Dân trí, 20 Sep 2026](https://dantri.com.vn/lao-dong-viec-lam/hang-loat-vi-tri-viec-lam-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat-tai-tphcm-20260919181339133.htm)

### Cited Findings: global comparison
- **US (AFB, 2025 data, ages 16–64):**

  | Indicator | Blind or VI | No visual impairment |
  |---|---|---|
  | Labour force participation | 47% | 75% |
  | Employment-to-population ratio | about 40% | about 71% |
  | Unemployment | 10% | 4% |

  — [AFB Employment Statistics](https://afb.org/research-and-initiatives/statistics/employment-bvi)
- **US (NRTC / BRFSS and ACS, ages 18–64):** about 8.7 million people report blindness or low vision, and 43.6% of them work, against 70.4% of those without. — [NRTC fact sheet, Mississippi State, 2024](https://www.blind.msstate.edu/sites/www.blind.msstate.edu/files/2024-12/Employment%20Statistics%20for%20Adults%20with%20Visual%20Impairments.pdf); [APH ConnectCenter](https://aphconnectcenter.org/employment/sobering-job-stats-adults-blind-low-vision/)
- **UK (RNIB):**
  - **Employment rates:** people registered blind or partially sighted **27%**; general population 76%; disabled people excluding sight loss 51%. The rate "has not changed in a generation" and has fallen since 2018.
  - **Barriers they report:** inaccessible recruitment (36%), employer attitudes (35%), poor employer support (32%).
  - **Qualifications:** blind or partially sighted people with degrees have the same chance of a job as sighted people with no qualifications.
  - — [RNIB employment report 2022](https://www.rnib.org.uk/professionals/research-and-data/reports-and-insight/employment-of-people-with-sight-loss-in-2022/); [RNIB news](https://www.rnib.org.uk/news/reversing-the-trend-in-employment-of-people-with-sight-loss/). These figures come from search-result summaries of RNIB pages; the fetched page did not render the numbers.

### Inferences
- Vietnam's VI employment rate (≤10% by the NSO type-of-disability breakdown) looks far below the UK (27%) and US (about 40–44%). The definitions differ: Vietnam uses functional difficulty in a household survey, the UK uses registered blindness, and the US uses self-reported difficulty. Present this as indicative, not a like-for-like comparison.
- Barriers are about **access to tools and training** (internet use 33.6% against 83.7%; vocational training 8.8% against 25.4%) and **employer attitudes and recruitment processes** (RNIB). This supports a digital, employer-facing intervention.
- Occupational concentration in massage and handicraft, with average income of 2.3–3.6 million VND, sits below regional minimum wages. The 2026 regional minimum wage is not verified here, so check it before citing.

### Gaps
- I found no official Vietnam labour-force breakdown for VI people specifically. I found no survey of VI people in office or IT jobs in Vietnam.
- I found no ILO Vietnam figure specific to VI employment.
- The VI-specific rate from NSO 2023 comes from a search summary. Confirm the exact percentage in the full PDF.

## Q3. What is Vietnam's legal framework for disabled people's employment and digital accessibility (CRPD, PWD Law 2010, Labor Code 2019, Decree 28/2012, Circular 26/2020, 2025–2026 updates)?

### Takeaway
Vietnam has **no hiring quota for the private sector**. It relies on non-discrimination, duties on employers, and **incentives for enterprises where at least 30% of workers are disabled** (the corporate income tax exemption was removed in 2013). The key **2026 update** is the Employment Law 2025: from 1 Jan 2026 employers can pay reduced unemployment insurance contributions for 12 months for newly hired disabled workers. Government websites must meet WCAG 2.0 under Circular 26/2020, but enforcement is weak.

### Cited Findings
- **UN CRPD:** Vietnam ratified on **5 Feb 2015**, authorised by National Assembly Resolution 84/2014/QH13 of 28 Nov 2014. — [UNDP/UNPRPD Situational Analysis Viet Nam, 2023](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2023-03/SA%20report%20En%20short.pdf) (via search summary)
- **Law on Persons with Disabilities 2010 (51/2010/QH12), Article 34:** enterprises using **≥30% disabled workers** receive:
  - support to adapt working conditions;
  - preferential loans from the Vietnam Bank for Social Policies;
  - priority in leasing land and premises;
  - an exemption from land rent where **≥70%** of workers are disabled.
  - Details are set out in **Decree 28/2012/NĐ-CP** (10 Apr 2012). — [Decree 28/2012 on thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Van-hoa-Xa-hoi/Nghi-dinh-28-2012-ND-CP-huong-dan-Luat-nguoi-khuyet-tat-137918.aspx); [Chinhphu.vn](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=157455)
- The **corporate income tax exemption** in Article 34 was **removed** by Law 32/2013/QH13 (effective 1 Jan 2014). — [Consolidated text 24/VBHN-VPQH, 26 Feb 2026, via LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/chinh-sach/van-ban-hop-nhat-24-vbhn-vpqh-2026-luat-nguoi-khuyet-tat-427350-d5.html)
- **Consolidated PWD Law, 26 Feb 2026:**
  - It folds in Law 32/2013 and the Law on Planning 112/2025/QH15 (effective 1 Mar 2026).
  - The 2025 change only affects Article 50 wording.
  - The employment articles (32–35) are largely unchanged.
  - — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/chinh-sach/van-ban-hop-nhat-24-vbhn-vpqh-2026-luat-nguoi-khuyet-tat-427350-d5.html)
- **Pending amendment:** a draft law amending several laws, including the Law on Persons with Disabilities (with the Laws on Children, Medical Examination and Treatment, Pharmaceuticals and others), was added to the **2026 legislative programme**. It is due for adoption at the **October 2026 session**. The focus is administrative: two-level local government, decentralisation and cutting procedures. It is not a comprehensive rewrite of disability rights. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/bo-sung-2-du-an-luat-lon-vao-chuong-trinh-lap-phap-2026-trinh-quoc-hoi-thong-qua-thang-10-2026-186-110932-article.html); [Báo Văn hóa](https://baovanhoa.vn/chinh-tri/uy-ban-thuong-vu-quoc-hoi-cho-y-kien-du-an-luat-sua-doi-bo-sung-5-luat-thuoc-linh-vuc-y-te-xa-hoi-262017.html)
- **Labor Code 2019 (45/2019/QH14):**
  - **Article 159:** employers must ensure working conditions, tools and occupational safety suitable for disabled workers, and must consult them on decisions that affect their rights.
  - **Article 160** prohibits two things unless the worker consents: overtime or night work for workers with a mild disability and ≥51% loss of working capacity, or with a severe or especially severe disability; and heavy, hazardous or dangerous work without the worker's informed consent.
  - — [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/lao-dong-tien-luong/6-luu-y-khi-su-dung-lao-dong-la-nguoi-khuyet-tat-la-gi-13730.html); [hethongphapluat, Article 160](https://hethongphapluat.com/bo-luat-lao-dong-2019/dieu-160)
- **Employment Law 2025 (74/2025/QH15):**
  - **Passage:** adopted 16 Jun 2025 (455 of 459 votes), with 8 chapters and 55 articles.
  - **Effective date:** **1 Jan 2026**.
  - **New incentive:** employers get a reduction in unemployment insurance contributions for **newly hired disabled workers for up to 12 months**. The implementing decree proposes cutting the employer rate from 0.9% to **0%**, at an estimated cost of about 129.6 billion VND a year.
  - **Clawback:** employers who end the contract early must repay the reduction.
  - — [Tuổi Trẻ / Saigon Times, 27 Aug 2025](https://tuoitre.vn/saigontimes/tu-1-1-2026-doanh-nghiep-giam-dong-bao-hiem-that-nghiep-cho-lao-dong-khuyet-tat/); [Chinhphu.vn](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/quoc-hoi-thong-qua-luat-viec-lam-sua-doi-119250616114808013.htm); [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/phap-luat-doanh-nghiep/bai-viet/du-thao-luat-viec-lam-2025-sua-doi-da-duoc-quoc-hoi-thong-qua-co-hieu-luc-tu-ngay-01-01-2026-12813.html)
- **Digital accessibility, Circular 26/2020/TT-BTTTT (effective 1 Jan 2021):**
  - Websites, portals and public-service portals of ministries, government agencies and People's Committees at all levels must apply accessibility standards and technologies for PWD.
  - **WCAG 2.0** is the referenced standard.
  - Existing sites had 12 months to upgrade.
  - — [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Thong-tu-26-2020-TT-BTTTT-ap-dung-cong-nghe-ho-tro-nguoi-khuyet-tat-tiep-can-san-pham-thong-tin-453691.aspx); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/thong-tu-26-2020-tt-btttt-ho-tro-nguoi-khuyet-tat-su-dung-dich-vu-thong-tin-va-truyen-thong-191327-d1.html); [MST](https://mst.gov.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/14587.htm)
- Tia Sáng reports that online public services remain hard for PWD to use in practice. — [Tia Sáng](https://tiasang.com.vn/nguoi-khuyet-tat-dich-vu-cong-online-sao-kho-den-vay-5086534.html) (title only; not fetched)

### Inferences
- The legal "carrot" for mainstream (not segregated) employers is small: 12 months of unemployment insurance relief plus the Labor Code duty to accommodate. The ≥30% threshold mostly suits social enterprises and massage centres. A hackathon product that lowers the practical cost of hiring one VI worker in an ordinary firm fits the legal gap.
- Circular 26/2020 covers **public-sector** websites only (WCAG 2.0, which is outdated). I found no WCAG mandate for private-sector recruitment platforms or HR tools.

### Gaps
- I did not find the exact article number in Law 74/2025 for the unemployment insurance reduction, or the final implementing decree number and rate.
- I found no evidence of a private-sector hiring quota. An older quota (2–3% under the 1998 Ordinance) was dropped in the 2010 Law; this is from memory and was not verified in this session, so do not cite it without checking.
- Circular 26/2020 may have been replaced after the MIC merged into the Ministry of Science and Technology (MST) in 2025. I did not verify whether it is still in force or updated to WCAG 2.1 or 2.2.
- I did not fetch or verify the article-level text of PWD Law Articles 32–35.

## Q4. Which Vietnamese organisations work on VI education and employment, and what job fairs and portals exist?

### Takeaway
The ecosystem centres on the state-backed **Vietnam Blind Association** (mass organisation, massage and handicraft jobs), the NGO **Sao Mai Center** (assistive tech, IT training, job placement) and **DRD** in HCMC (disability rights, a 2025 AI job app with UIT). There are also annual PWD job fairs in HCMC.

### Cited Findings
- **Vietnam Blind Association:** 72,697 members. It runs 371 production facilities (270 massage), vocational classes and livelihood loans (over 53 billion VND from central sources plus 21.5 billion VND from local sources). Its 2025–2030 target is 70% of working-age blind people in stable jobs. — [Nhân Dân, Dec 2025](https://nhandan.vn/phan-dau-tao-viec-lam-nang-cao-chat-luong-doi-song-cho-nguoi-mu-post930923.html); [Nhân Dân, Feb 2025](https://nhandan.vn/ty-le-lao-dong-mu-tham-gia-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-con-thap-post861853.html)
- **Sao Mai Center** (Trung tâm Hướng nghiệp & Công nghệ Trợ giúp cho Người mù Sao Mai, HCMC):
  - Founded **2001** as a non-profit.
  - Work: assistive technology for education and employment, computer and music training, orientation and mobility, vocational guidance, training and **job placement**.
  - A network of **26 computer rooms in 23 provinces**.
  - The "Capacity Building Through Employment" project for VI people has run since **2024**.
  - — [Sao Mai Center: Employment](https://saomaicenter.org/vi/employment); [Sao Mai Center (EN)](https://saomaicenter.org/en); [DRD article on Sao Mai](https://www.drdvietnam.org/vi/tin-bon-phuong/2046-trung-tam-tin-hoc-vi-nguoi-mu-sao-mai.html)
- **DRD** (Disability Research & Capacity Development / Trung tâm Khuyết tật và Phát triển, HCMC): founded 3 Dec 2005. On 14 Oct 2025 it received a PWD **employment app (vieclamnkt.top)** built with the University of Information Technology (UIT, VNU-HCM), described as AI-supported. — [UIT](https://www.uit.edu.vn/bai-viet/uit-va-drd-dong-hanh-phat-trien-ung-dung-ai-ho-tro-viec-lam-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat); [DRD](https://drdvietnam.org/)
- **HCMC job fair for PWD, 18 Sep 2025:** 56 units took part with over 3,000 vacancies, including **28 firms offering 522 positions specifically for PWD**. Nearly 500 people with disabilities attended. — [NLĐ, Sep 2025](https://nld.com.vn/tp-hcm-tuyen-dung-hon-3000-vi-tri-mo-rong-co-hoi-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat-196250918160910264.htm); [HTV](https://htv.com.vn/tphcm-hon-500-co-hoi-viec-lam-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat-222250919110238806.htm); [PLO](https://plo.vn/ngay-hoi-viec-lam-2025-xoa-bo-dinh-kien-voi-lao-dong-la-nguoi-khuyet-tat-post870962.html)
- **HCMC job fair for PWD, 23 Sep 2026:**
  - **Organisers:** the HCMC Employment Service Center, the Center for Support, Vocational Training and Job Creation, the HCMC Youth Employment Service Center, and the Xuân Hòa Ward People's Committee.
  - **Roles named for VI workers:** telephone customer service, customer care and sales.
  - — [Dân trí, 20 Sep 2026](https://dantri.com.vn/lao-dong-viec-lam/hang-loat-vi-tri-viec-lam-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat-tai-tphcm-20260919181339133.htm)
- **ACDC** (Action to the Community Development Center) hosts the national disability survey report in its library. — [ACDC](https://acdc.vn/vi/bao-cao-dieu-tra-quoc-gia-ve-nguoi-khuyet-tat-vipdf-841.html)

### Inferences
- The job-matching pipeline for VI people is fragmented: association-run massage and craft jobs, NGO training, and occasional job fairs. The DRD/UIT app shows interest in digital matching, but I found nothing specific to VI or accessibility-first. That is a possible positioning gap for a hackathon.

## Q5. What is the business case: cost of accommodations, retention, and financial performance?

### Takeaway
Most accommodations are cheap: **61% cost nothing and the median one-time cost is US$300** (JAN, 2019–2024). Employers report **85% better retention**. Accenture finds that disability-inclusion leaders earn more revenue and profit.

### Cited Findings
- **JAN employer survey (Jan 2019–Dec 2024):**
  - **Sample:** 26,028 employers surveyed, 5,406 responses; 1,425 answered the cost questions.
  - **Cost:** **61%** of accommodations cost nothing. **33%** had a one-time cost, **median US$300**. 6% had ongoing costs, median US$2,400 a year.
  - **Direct benefits employers reported:** retained a valued employee (**85%**), increased productivity (52%), avoided the cost of training a new employee (48%), better attendance (47%), and savings on workers' compensation or insurance (24%).
  - **Effectiveness:** 66% of 2,069 employers rated the accommodations very or extremely effective.
  - — [JAN, Costs and Benefits of Accommodation](https://askjan.org/topics/costs.cfm)
- US DOL (2023): nearly half of accommodations have no cost. — [US DOL, May 2023](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/odep/odep20230504)
- **Accenture "Getting to Equal" (2018, with Disability:IN and AAPD):** 45 disability-inclusion leaders had, on average over 4 years, **28% higher revenue, double the net income and 30% higher economic profit margins** than other companies. — [Accenture newsroom, 2018](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2018/companies-leading-in-disability-inclusion-have-outperformed-peers-accenture-research-finds)
- **Accenture 2023 update:** leaders had **1.6x more revenue, 2.6x more net income and 2x more economic profit**, and were 25% more likely to outperform industry peers on productivity. — [Accenture newsroom, 2023](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2023/companies-that-lead-in-disability-inclusion-outperform-peers-financially-reveals-new-research-from-accenture); [Forbes, Nov 2023](https://www.forbes.com/sites/gusalexiou/2023/11/30/latest-accenture-research-confirms-disability-inclusive-companies-are-more-profitable/)

### Inferences
- For VI workers, common accommodations are free screen readers (NVDA, TalkBack, VoiceOver) and accessible document and software practices. These fall mostly in JAN's "$0" band. Combined with Vietnam's 12-month unemployment insurance relief, the cost barrier for Vietnamese employers is mostly perception, not money. This is an inference; I found no Vietnam-specific cost study.
- The Accenture studies are correlational and US-centric, so frame them as "association" and not causation.
